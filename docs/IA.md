# Kindline Care Foundation – Website Information Architecture (IA)

## Global Layout

### Primary Navigation
- Home
- About Us
- Programs
- Get Involved
- Impact & Stories
- News / Blog
- Donate (Primary CTA)

### Global Elements
- Header with logo (royal blue dominant)
- Footer with:
  - Quick links
  - Contact information
  - Social media links
  - Donation CTA
  - Copyright & NGO disclaimer

---

## 1. Home Page

### Purpose
Introduce the foundation, communicate trust and compassion, and drive user engagement (donate, volunteer, sponsor).

### Sections

#### Hero Section
- Headline: Short mission-driven statement
- Tagline: **“My care. Your care. Our care.”**
- Primary CTA: Donate
- Secondary CTA: Get Involved
- Background: Human-centered imagery (children, widows, communities)

#### About Snapshot
- Brief overview of Kindline Care Foundation
- Link to full About page

#### Programs Overview
- Cards for:
  - WESAP
  - Child Support & Development
  - Community Development
- Short descriptions
- Link to Programs page

#### Impact Highlights
- Key stats (e.g. lives impacted, programs delivered)
- Link to Impact & Stories page

#### Get Involved Call-to-Action
- Volunteer
- Partner
- Donate
- Sponsor a Child/Widow

#### Latest News
- Recent blog posts or announcements
- Link to News page

## Design & UX Notes

- Royal blue as the primary brand color
- Calm, elegant spacing and typography
- Accessible components (Radix UI)
- Strong CTAs with emotional clarity
- Human-centered imagery over stock visuals
