# Programs Page

## Header
- **Headline:** Our Programs
- **Subtitle:** Empowering lives through targeted, community-based interventions.

## Program 1: WESAP (Widows Economic Skills Advancement Program.)
- **Category:** Economic Empowerment
- **Description:** WESAP is a structured program designed to equip widows with practical and market-driven skills that enable them to generate income, become self-sufficient economically and support themselves and their families.
- **Key Offerings:**
  - Entrepreneurship, Farming & Business Skills
  - Financial Literacy
  - Enterprise Circles (Peer Support Groups)

## Program 2: Child Support & Development
- **Category:** Child Welbeing
- **Description:** We are dedicated to ensuring that every child has the chance to succeed. Our comprehensive program offers education support, literacy development, nutrition, and wellbeing for orphans and vulnerable children, enabling them to become confident and responsible members of their communities.
- **Education Focus:**
  - Enrolment Support
  - School uniforms and shoes
  - School supplies (books, pens, bags)
- **Healthcare Focus:**
  - Nutrition & Well-being - Meal kits for home
  - Counselling & life skills sessions
  - Health & hygiene guidance

## Program 3: Community Development
- **Category:** Sustainable Impact
- **Description:** True change happens when communities are empowered to lead. We collaborate with local leaders and stakeholders to build capacity and sustainable initiatives that benefit the entire community.
- **Focus Areas:** Water and sanitation projects, community health outreach, and infrastructure improvement.
