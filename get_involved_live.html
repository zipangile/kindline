Love Care Share

Friends of Kindline

Love Care Share is Kindline Care Foundation's giving community for individuals, families, faith-Based and organizations who commit to support widows, orphans, and vulnerable children through monthly, quarterly, or annual giving.

Join Our Community

Why Join Love Care Share?

Being a Friend of the Foundation means you are the backbone of our work. Your consistent commitment allows us to plan ahead, respond to emergencies, and guarantee long-term support for those who depend on us.

Consistent Impact

Monthly giving ensures no child is left without school requirements and no widow without support.

Community Connection

Receive exclusive updates, field reports, and invitations to special community events.

Be the backbone of our work

Your recurring support creates lasting change and transforms lives.

Your Gift, Their Future.

Every kwacha you give helps a child access education and empowers a widow to rebuild her life with dignity, hope, and independence.

Here is exactly what your support could do:

K750 / $40 — School Supplies for one Child

Could provide essential school supplies including books, uniform, shoes, and a school bag; giving a child the chance to learn with confidence.

K1500 / $80 — Business Starter Kit

Could empower a widow with the tools, resources, and basic training needed to start a small, sustainable business.

K1000 / $50 — Skills Training Support

Could support widows’ meetings within the community, where women receive skills training, encouragement, and practical support to rebuild their lives and support their families.
